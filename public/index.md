# Find a Doc, Japan

Find a Doc, Japan is a free directory of clinics and healthcare professionals in Japan. You can filter by the languages the staff actually speak, by medical specialty, and by prefecture and city.

The publisher is NPO法人Find a Doc, Japan, a specified non-profit certified in Tokyo on 28 August 2023. Registration number 9011005010215. Contact: contact@findadoc.jp.

This directory does not provide medical advice, a diagnosis, or a booking. Use a clinic or doctor page to find the clinic's own phone number, website, or map link, and contact the clinic for care.

## How to read a page

- Clinic URLs look like `/clinic/{prefecture}/{city}/{name}--{id}`.
- Doctor URLs look like `/doctor/{name}--{id}`.
- Prefecture and city hubs look like `/tokyo` and `/tokyo/shibuya`.
- Specialty and language hubs look like `/tokyo/dentistry` and `/tokyo/english-speaking`.
- The id after `--` is stable. The slug is a romanised name and can change.

Other languages use a prefix such as `/ja-JP/clinic/...`. English has no prefix. Hreflang on each page lists every locale.

The full URL list is https://www.findadoc.jp/sitemap.xml. Agent instructions are https://www.findadoc.jp/llms.txt.
