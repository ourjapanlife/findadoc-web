# About Find a Doc, Japan

Find a Doc, Japan connects people living in Japan with healthcare services in a language they speak. The directory is free. Clinics and doctors are suggested by the public and checked by moderators before they are published.

The organisation is a registered specified non-profit, NPO法人Find a Doc, Japan, certified by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government on 28 August 2023. The registration number is 9011005010215. Filed documents, including the balance sheet, are on https://www.findadoc.jp/npo.

Anyone can suggest a clinic or a doctor at https://www.findadoc.jp/submit. Corrections to a page that is already up go to contact@findadoc.jp. The project also runs a volunteer programmer community: https://github.com/ourjapanlife and https://docs.findadoc.jp.

The site does not practise medicine. A listing is a pointer to a clinic, with the languages that clinic's staff speak, the specialties on the record, and the contact details the clinic published. For an appointment or a medical question, contact the clinic.

English is the default URL. Japanese and the other site languages are prefixes on the same path, for example https://www.findadoc.jp/ja-JP/about.
